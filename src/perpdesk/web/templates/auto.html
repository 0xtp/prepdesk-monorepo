Shared rules

  • Size: each trade risks {{ st.auto_risk_pct }}% of that bot's balance if stopped; leverage only as needed, max {{ st.auto_max_leverage }}x isolated; liquidation must sit well beyond the stop.
  • Limits per bot: max {{ st.auto_max_positions }} positions, one per coin; −{{ st.auto_daily_loss_pct }}% in a day pauses that bot until tomorrow; −{{ st.auto_max_drawdown_pct }}% from its peak pauses it until you resume. The kill switch stops all bots.
  • Fills: market entry right after the 4h close (skipped if noticed more than {{ st.auto_max_entry_delay_min }} min late), real fees and slippage; if a stop and a target fall inside the same candle, the stop counts.
  • Coins: breakout {{ st.auto_coins|join(', ') }}; other bots {{ st.lab_coins|join(', ') }}.
  • Funding carry assumes you can hold spot of the same coin (on Hyperliquid spot or elsewhere).
