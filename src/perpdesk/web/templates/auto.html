Rules

  • Entry: a 4h candle closes above its {{ st.auto_breakout_lookback }}-bar high on above-average volume and the overall analysis is also LONG. Market entry right after the close (skipped if noticed more than {{ st.auto_max_entry_delay_min }} min late).
  • Exit: stop below structure; 50% off at TP1, then stop to break-even; rest at TP2; anything left closes after {{ st.max_hold_hours }}h.
  • Size: {{ st.auto_risk_pct }}% of equity lost if stopped; leverage only as needed, max {{ st.auto_max_leverage }}x isolated; liquidation must sit well beyond the stop.
  • Limits: max {{ st.auto_max_positions }} positions, one per coin; −{{ st.auto_daily_loss_pct }}% in a day pauses until tomorrow; −{{ st.auto_max_drawdown_pct }}% from peak halts until you resume.
  • Coins: {{ st.auto_coins|join(', ') }}. BTC and AVAX are excluded because this setup lost money on them in testing.

Change these under Settings (AUTO_*). Paper fills use the backtest's conservative rules: if the stop and a target fall inside the same 1-minute candle, the stop counts.
